# Support

Nightpass: Satellite Tracker

**Need help?** Email [mehmetsagirdev@gmail.com](mailto:mehmetsagirdev@gmail.com) and describe what you were doing when the problem appeared. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version shown in Settings. Replies usually go out within two business days.

## I can't see the pass the app showed

Passes are only visible when the sky is dark where you are but the satellite is still lit by the sun, usually in the first hours after sunset and before sunrise. Look in the direction shown at the start time and give your eyes a minute to adjust. Clouds, city lights and a low pass close to the horizon all make it harder.

## The pass times look wrong

Check that your location is correct on the home screen. Orbits change slightly every day, so open the app before you go out; it downloads fresh orbit data automatically when you are online.

## I'm not getting pass alerts

Make sure notifications are allowed for Nightpass in _Settings → Notifications_. With the cloud filter on, alerts are skipped when your sky is forecast to be overcast.

## The camera view points the wrong way

Move your phone in a figure-eight to recalibrate the compass, and keep it away from magnets and metal surfaces.

## I subscribed but the app still shows the paywall

Open Settings and tap **Restore purchases**. Subscriptions belong to the Apple Account that bought them, so make sure the device is signed in to that account.

## How do I cancel my subscription?

Open _Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions_ on your iPhone, or Settings → Manage subscription inside Nightpass. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge. Refunds are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## What does Pro include?

Every visible pass (the free version shows ISS passes), automatic pass alerts, the cloud filter, live AR sky tracking, launch visibility, Live Activities, an unlimited collection, up to 5 saved places and all share card themes. Pro is available weekly, monthly or yearly; the yearly plan starts with a free trial.

## Requirements

iPhone with iOS 18 or later. The Apple Watch app needs watchOS 11 or later. No account is needed.
