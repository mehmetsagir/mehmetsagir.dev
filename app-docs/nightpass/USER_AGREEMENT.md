## Accuracy

Pass times, directions, brightness, cloud forecasts and launch visibility are predictions based on public orbit data, launch schedules and weather forecasts. Orbits change, launches slip and weather is uncertain, so predictions can be wrong. Nightpass is for observing the sky for fun and learning; do not rely on it for navigation, safety or any critical purpose.
