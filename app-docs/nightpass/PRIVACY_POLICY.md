# Privacy Policy

Nightpass — last updated 24 November 2026

Nightpass tells you which satellites, space stations and rockets are passing over you, and when to look up. This policy describes what the app uses, where it goes and what stays on your device.

**Nightpass collects no data.** There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

## Your location

- Nightpass needs your location to calculate which objects pass over you. The calculation runs entirely on your iPhone.
- To show the name of your place (for example "Istanbul"), your coordinates are passed to Apple's geocoding service in iOS.
- For the cloud filter, your coordinates are passed to Apple Weather (WeatherKit) to get the cloud forecast for your sky.
- Apple handles these requests under the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Your location is never sent to us or to any other company.
- If you prefer not to share your location, you can pick a place manually instead.

## Data the app downloads

Nightpass downloads public orbit data from [CelesTrak](https://celestrak.org) and launch schedules from [Launch Library 2 by The Space Devs](https://thespacedevs.com). These requests contain no personal information and no location; they are the same for every user.

## Camera and motion

The camera and motion sensors are used only while you point your phone at the sky, to show where to look. Nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded.

## What stays on your device

- **Saved places** — the places you choose to follow.
- **Your collection** — the passes you marked as seen, and the badges you earned.
- **Settings and alerts** — your preferences and scheduled pass notifications.
- **Cached data** — the latest orbit and launch data, so the app works offline.

This data is shared with the app's widgets and Apple Watch app on your own devices. Deleting the app removes all of it.

## Notifications

Pass alerts are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No push server is involved.

## Payments

Nightpass Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details or your Apple Account information.

## Children

Nightpass does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes with it.
