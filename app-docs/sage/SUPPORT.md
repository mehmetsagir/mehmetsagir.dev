# Support

Sage — AI Notes

**Need help?** Email [memetsagire@gmail.com](mailto:memetsagire@gmail.com) and describe what you were doing when the problem appeared. Include your device (iPhone, iPad or Mac) and the app version from Settings → Version. Replies usually go out within two business days.

## I paid for Pro but the app still shows the paywall

Open Settings and tap **Restore purchases**. Purchases are tied to the Apple Account that made them, so make sure the device is signed in to that account. One subscription covers iPhone, iPad and Mac.

## How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by us, so they are cancelled in _Settings → your name → Subscriptions_ on iOS, or _App Store → your name → Account Settings_ on macOS. Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues until the period you already paid for ends.

## Can you recover a note I deleted?

No. Note bodies are encrypted on your device before they reach our servers and the key never leaves your account, so we cannot read or restore your content — not even to help. Deletion is permanent.

## How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. It removes your profile, workspaces, notes and sign-in identity immediately, and cannot be undone.

## Which languages does Sage support?

Fifteen, plus English: Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Dutch, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Polish, Ukrainian and Indonesian. Sage follows the system language, and you can override it in Settings → App language.

## Requirements

iOS 18 or later, or macOS 15 or later. An account is required so your notes can sync between your devices.

What Sage stores, and what it cannot see, is described in the [privacy policy](/sage/privacy-policy).
