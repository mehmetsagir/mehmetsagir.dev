## How do I end a showing?

Press and hold the lock in the top-right corner, then confirm with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. If Strict Lock is on, triple-click the side button and unlock Guided Access instead; the showing ends with it.

## Triple-clicking the side button does nothing

Guided Access is turned off. Turn it on in _Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access_ and set a passcode there. Peekproof's Settings → Set Up Guided Access walks you through it.

## Can someone still leave the app?

Without Strict Lock, iOS lets anyone swipe home, as with any app; Peekproof then hides the photos and asks for your Face ID when it is opened again. With Strict Lock on, Guided Access keeps the phone inside Peekproof until you unlock it.

## I bought Pro but the app still shows the paywall

Open Settings and tap **Restore Purchases**. Purchases belong to the Apple Account that made them, so make sure the device is signed in to that account.

## What does Pro include?

Unlimited photos per showing (the free version shows up to 10), videos and Live Photos, unlimited saved sets, your own end card message and sharing the nosy report. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
