# Privacy Policy

Peekproof — last updated 24 November 2026

Peekproof lets you hand your phone to someone and show only the photos you picked. This policy describes what the app stores and where.

**Peekproof collects no data.** There is no account, no server, no analytics and no tracking. The app makes no network requests of its own, and nothing you do in it is sent to us or to anyone else.

## Your photos

- **Choosing photos** uses Apple's system photo picker. Peekproof only receives the photos and videos you select, and choosing them does not require access to your photo library.
- **While you show them**, the selected items are copied to a temporary folder inside the app on your device. That folder is deleted when the showing ends.
- **Photo library access** is requested only if you use the "Show recent photos" shortcut or saved sets. It is used on your device to read the photos you asked to show, and is never uploaded.

## What stays on your device

- **Saved sets** — the name and emoji you give a set, and the identifiers of the photos in it (not copies of the photos).
- **Settings** — your preferences, shared with the app's Share and Control Center extensions on the same device.
- **Showing statistics** — how many showings you have run and how many extra swipes and exit attempts were counted. These numbers are not linked to any person or photo.
- **Exit PIN** — if your device has no passcode and you create a PIN, only a salted hash of it is kept in the iOS Keychain.

Deleting the app removes all of it.

## Face ID and Touch ID

Unlocking the exit of a showing uses Apple's Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. Authentication happens entirely in iOS; Peekproof only learns whether it succeeded and never has access to your biometric data.

## Payments

Peekproof Pro is a one-time purchase through Apple. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details or your Apple Account information.

## Children

Peekproof does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes with it.
